Class III English Revision

Topics: Nouns, verbs, adjectives, punctuation, and reading comprehension.

Remember

  • A noun names a person, place, animal, or thing.
  • A verb shows an action or state.
  • An adjective describes a noun.
  • Begin sentences with a capital letter and end with correct punctuation.

Practice

  1. Underline the noun: The bird sings.
  2. Choose an adjective for a tall tree.
  3. Add punctuation: where are you going
