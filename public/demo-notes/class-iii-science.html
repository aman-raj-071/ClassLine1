Class III Science Revision

Topics: Plants, animals, materials, and healthy habits.

Key ideas

  • Plants need air, water, light, and nutrients to grow.
  • Animals need food, water, shelter, and space.
  • Materials can be solid, liquid, or gas.
  • Handwashing and balanced meals help us stay healthy.

Try this

  1. Name three things a plant needs.
  2. Sort a spoon, milk, and air by state of matter.
  3. Write two healthy daily habits.
