Topics: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, and time.

Remember

  • Line up numbers by place value before adding or subtracting.
  • Multiplication is repeated addition.
  • A fraction shows equal parts of one whole.
  • Read the hour hand first when telling time.
